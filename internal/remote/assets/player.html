The radio itself

Stops the radio for everyone: nothing plays and nothing is generated, so a machine left on is not spending its graphics card on music nobody is hearing. What is already made is kept, and waking starts from it.

Throws away every song the radio has made ahead and starts generating again from one quick song, then a small batch, then deeper ones as the sound settles. The station, its steering and your saved songs are untouched. This is for when the songs already waiting in the buffer are not the ones you want to hear.

Playback on this device

Downloads whole tracks ahead of time, so a dead zone does not interrupt the music.

Applies to the live stream while buffered playback is on, and to your saved songs either way — so a saved song starts on the tap instead of loading first, and the next one is ready before it is needed.

Keeps a few saved songs ready while you are on the live stream, and a few live songs ready while you are on the saved ones, so switching between them away from a good signal plays straight away. Off by default, because it spends data on music you may not listen to.

Six soft beeps when the radio stops delivering new music and starts repeating itself, so a stuck stream is heard rather than discovered an hour later.

Only when the car asks to play or you tap - never on a timer, so a phone in a pocket stays silent. For hands-free resume, turn on “Automatically resume media” in Android Auto.

Saving

An endless stream has no previous track, so the car's previous-track button saves what you are hearing instead. The icon itself cannot be relabelled.

Vocals

Each song picks a language at random from the ones you list here, and the switches on the Live screen narrow that down. Saving drops the tracks queued ahead and starts generating in the new languages. Leave it empty to let the music engine sing in whatever language it likes.
